Everything is computed in your browser. The same physics lives in Python as mechanics.pendulum_period, quantum.barrier_transmission and quantum.double_slit_experiment, where it is checked against these analytic results by the test suite.

The tunnelling plot is the exact stationary state, matched across both barrier edges. The double-slit pattern uses the Fraunhofer (far-field) formula.
