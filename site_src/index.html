Accuracy

Pretty pictures are easy. Correct ones are the point.

Every function is tested against an analytic result, a conservation law or a limiting case. Never against its own output.

Probability conserved

In tunnelling, transmitted plus reflected equals one to twelve digits.

10−8

Hydrogen, exactly

Radial functions normalised, and ⟨r⟩ = a₀(3n² − l(l+1))/2, to eight decimal places.

±0.01

Onsager, by Monte Carlo
